01

Empathize

Housing instability rarely comes from one event. It builds up from rent pressure, thin savings, unpredictable informal-sector income, and few people to fall back on. This tool walks through those factors the way a household actually experiences them.

02

Define

We define the problem as: "What is the probability a household experiences a housing crisis (missed rent, forced move, or eviction) in the next 12 months, given their income, housing costs, and support network?"

03

Ideate

Research on housing instability consistently points to a handful of levers: rent burden, savings buffer, job formality, and prior warning signs (late payments, eviction notices). The prototype below turns those levers into sliders you can move.
